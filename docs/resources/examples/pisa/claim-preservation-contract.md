# Completed example — Claim-Preservation Contract

**Project:** PISA moving-benchmark analysis  
**Research object / manuscript:** Beyond PISA rankings  
**Analysis version:** Mature empirical specification  
**Contract status:** Retrospective representation of the authorised claim boundary

## 1. Verified evidence

| Result / object ID | Verified statement | Source / location | Uncertainty / qualification |
|---|---|---|---|
| EV-PC1 | PC1 explains approximately 97.31% of variation in the three published domain means | Frozen comparator output | Describes dominant global variation |
| EV-BM | Ball Mapper provides overlapping local neighbourhood/profile structure | Frozen Ball Mapper outputs | Sensitive to construction choices; not a replacement for global summary |
| EV-TRAJ | Ordered chain among 36 systems: 21 downward, 14 unchanged, 1 upward | Frozen trajectory output | Specific to identified ordered-state construction |
| EV-ROB | Across 250 landmark orders, median 19 downward, 14 unchanged, 1 upward | Robustness archive | Exact states vary; broad gradient persists |
| EV-UK4 | England, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland are analysed separately; aggregate UK excluded | Final analytical specification | PISA 2022 sampling caveat remains relevant |

## 2. Authorised claims

| Claim ID | Authorised claim boundary | Supported by | Permitted variation in wording |
|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | Rank movement, own-score movement and movement within a changing comparison field are non-equivalent | Empirical design + observed trajectories | May be restated without implying causality |
| C2 | PCA is the preferred compact global summary of the three-domain structure | EV-PC1 | "Preferred", "compact", "dominant global summary" |
| C3 | Ball Mapper complements PCA by representing local overlapping neighbourhood/profile relationships | EV-BM + comparator evidence | Must retain complementary/local meaning |
| C4 | Broad downward movement is robust across landmark-order sensitivity checks | EV-TRAJ + EV-ROB | Must retain robustness scope |
| C5 | The four UK administrations should be interpreted separately in this analysis | EV-UK4 | Must retain aggregate-UK exclusion rationale |

## 3. Prohibited claims

| Prohibited claim / claim class | Reason prohibited |
|---|---|
| Ball Mapper uncovers a fundamentally different global taxonomy that PCA misses | PC1 captures ~97.31% of global variation |
| 17.35 percentage points moved directly from B4 to B9 | Direct same-system coupling is not identified |
| Rank improvement demonstrates substantive educational improvement | Rank and own-score movement are non-equivalent |
| Robustness to landmark order establishes all forms of statistical uncertainty | Algorithmic sensitivity differs from survey/measurement uncertainty |
| The PISA case validates the reason-provenance framework | Case is developmental illustration, not validation |

## 4. Inferential boundaries

- Endpoint occupancy changes do not identify direct same-system flows between overlapping neighbourhoods.
- Comparator robustness does not remove survey sampling and measurement uncertainty.
- Descriptive international trajectories do not by themselves establish causal mechanisms.
- Local neighbourhood structure and global dimensionality answer different questions.

## 5. Required qualifications

| Claim ID | Required qualification |
|---|---|
| C2 | Global summary refers to the three published domain means |
| C3 | Ball Mapper is complementary rather than substitutive in this application |
| C4 | Robustness refers to the specified landmark-order analysis |
| C5 | PISA 2022 UK sampling-standard caveat remains visible |

## 6. Frozen conceptual distinctions

| Distinction | Must remain separate |
|---|---|
| Own-score change vs rank change | Do not use rank as a proxy for performance change |
| Global summary vs local neighbourhood structure | PCA and Ball Mapper answer different representational questions |
| Generated artefact vs verified empirical evidence | Code/text generation is not execution/verification |
| Endpoint occupancy vs direct flow | Do not infer unidentified coupling |
| Algorithmic robustness vs survey uncertainty | Do not collapse the two uncertainty sources |
