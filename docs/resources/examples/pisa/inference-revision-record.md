# Completed example — Inference Revision Record

**Project:** PISA moving-benchmark analysis  
**Inference ID:** INF-B4-B9-01

## Original result

Endpoint summaries showed a decline in occupancy of one Ball Mapper neighbourhood and an increase in another.

An early interpretation described a **17.35 percentage-point movement directly from B4 in 2018 to B9 in 2025**.

## Original interpretation

The wording treated the endpoint change as if it identified direct movement of the same education systems from B4 to B9.

## Identification question

Do the endpoint memberships identify a same-system coupling that permits direct B4→B9 flow to be inferred?

## Problem detected

- [ ] computation
- [ ] data
- [ ] representation
- [x] identification
- [ ] causal language
- [ ] generalisation
- [ ] uncertainty
- [ ] other

The endpoint summaries do not identify direct same-system movement between those two overlapping neighbourhoods.

## Corrected evidential statement

Observable changes in neighbourhood occupancy can be described, but they do not identify a direct same-system transfer from B4 to B9.

## Replacement analysis or evidence

The mature analysis uses the ordered-state trajectory among 36 systems:

- 21 move downward;
- 14 remain unchanged;
- 1 moves upward.

Across 250 fixed landmark orders, the median pattern is 19 downward, 14 unchanged and 1 upward among the chain systems.

## Manuscript consequence

The direct B4→B9 flow claim was removed.

The manuscript now describes endpoint-state movement and robustness without asserting an unidentified direct coupling.

## Provenance note

The original interpretation emerged during AI-assisted analytical development.

The identification problem was subsequently recognised through challenge and inspection, and the revised claim was authorised by the researcher.
