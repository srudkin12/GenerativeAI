## Why the challenge matters

If a simple low-dimensional summary captures almost all global variation, Ball Mapper should not be presented as discovering a fundamentally different global taxonomy.

## Test or evidence introduced

Principal component analysis and complexity-matched comparator constructions.

Key result:

- PC1 explains approximately **97.31%** of variation in the three published domain means.

Additional comparator results include strong, though incomplete, overlap between Ball Mapper and PC1-based structures.

## Result

The global structure is overwhelmingly dominated by one principal dimension.

Ball Mapper still provides a representation of local overlapping neighbourhood and profile relationships that is not identical to the global PCA summary.

## Final defensible claim

PCA provides a compact global summary of the dominant structure. Ball Mapper can complement that summary when local overlapping neighbourhood and profile relationships are substantively relevant.

## Residual uncertainty

The comparator analysis does not establish that Ball Mapper is uniquely necessary for every application with similar data.
