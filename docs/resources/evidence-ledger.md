## Warrant types

Use the category that actually licenses the proposition:

- academic literature;
- manuscript-generated evidence;
- authoritative primary evidence;
- transparent analytic reasoning.

## Contract status

- **Authorised** — within the current claim-preservation contract.
- **Prohibited** — exceeds or conflicts with the current authorised argument.
- **Requires reconsideration** — cannot be resolved as a presentation edit.

## Audit status

- **Pass** — proposition and wording remain supported.
- **Revise** — presentation defect can be repaired without changing the substantive claim.
- **Return to claim authorisation** — interpretation or boundary requires substantive reconsideration.
- **Return to analysis** — supporting evidence or analysis must change.
