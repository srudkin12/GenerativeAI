# Researcher resources

These resources are designed to be copied and adapted to another research project.

They are deliberately lightweight. The aim is to record **consequential decisions and claim boundaries**, not every prompt, keystroke or conversational exchange.

## Starting a project

### [AI-Assisted Research Charter](ai-assisted-research-charter.html)

Use before substantial AI-assisted exploration to state:

- the substantive research problem;
- the analytical object;
- non-negotiable constraints;
- permitted AI roles;
- verification boundaries;
- consequential decisions that should be recorded.

## Recording consequential decisions

### [AI-Assisted Research Decision Log](ai-assisted-research-decision-log.html)

Use when a decision materially changes the analytical object, scope, evidence base, interpretation or next stage of the research.

### [Reason-Provenance Record](reason-provenance-record.html)

Use when a later contribution or attribution claim may depend on reconstructing how a consequential move entered the research process.

### [Researcher–AI Contribution Map](researcher-ai-contribution-map.html)

Use to distinguish origination, evaluation, integration and interpretation across selected research episodes.

## Challenging claims

### [Claim Challenge Record](claim-challenge-record.html)

Use when a comparator, robustness check, reviewer, researcher or AI-assisted critique challenges a preferred claim.

### [Inference Revision Record](inference-revision-record.html)

Use when a result remains numerically or descriptively correct but its substantive interpretation changes, narrows or is withdrawn.

## Governing manuscript revision

### [Claim-Preservation Contract](claim-preservation-contract.html)

Use once the substantive argument is sufficiently mature to state:

- authorised claims;
- prohibited claims;
- inferential boundaries;
- required qualifications;
- frozen conceptual or terminological distinctions.

### [Evidence Ledger](evidence-ledger.html)

Use to connect substantive manuscript propositions to the evidence or reasoning that licenses them.

### [Post-Revision Sufficiency Audit](post-revision-sufficiency-audit.html)

Use after substantial compression, restructuring or AI-assisted rewriting to check that the revised manuscript still contains the evidence, qualifications and distinctions needed to support the authorised argument.

## Preparing material for release

### [Public Release Screening Checklist](public-release-screening-checklist.html)

Use before publishing process evidence, worked examples, slides or archival material.

---

## Suggested minimum set

You do not need every resource for every project.

A useful minimum sequence is:

**Research Charter → Decision Log → Claim Challenge Record → Claim-Preservation Contract → Release Screening Checklist**

Add the provenance, inference-revision and evidence-ledger resources when the research problem makes them useful.
