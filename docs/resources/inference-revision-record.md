# Inference Revision Record

**Project:**  
**Inference ID:**  
**Date:**  

## Original result

What quantity, pattern or output was observed?

## Original interpretation

What substantive meaning was attached to the result?

## Identification question

What must be true for that interpretation to be warranted?

## Problem detected

Was the problem with:

- [ ] computation;
- [ ] data;
- [ ] representation;
- [ ] identification;
- [ ] causal language;
- [ ] generalisation;
- [ ] uncertainty;
- [ ] other?

Explain.

## Corrected evidential statement

What does the result itself support?

## Replacement analysis or evidence

What alternative evidence was used after the original interpretation was narrowed or withdrawn?

## Manuscript consequence

Paste or summarise the change to the claim.

## Provenance note

Who or what proposed the original interpretation, identified the problem, evaluated the correction and integrated the revised claim?
