# Case 2 — Human contribution as specification, constraint and refusal

## Research setting

AI-assisted exploration created analytical possibilities, but the analytical object still had to be defined.

## Consequential moves

The researcher required the analysis to:

- treat England, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland as separate education systems from the outset;
- exclude aggregate UK to avoid double counting;
- re-test the Ball Mapper radius rather than inherit radius 50 as an unquestioned optimum;
- keep the PISA 2022 UK sampling caveat visible.

The radius-selection work later retained 50, but as a substantively interpretable member of a stable local regime rather than as an "optimal" value.

## Why this matters

Research contribution is not limited to inventing a new method or asking the first question.

A researcher can materially shape a project by deciding:

- what counts as an observation;
- which aggregation is substantively misleading;
- which inherited assumption needs to be reopened;
- which uncertainty must remain visible.

## Reason-provenance lesson

**Constraint and refusal can be intellectual contributions.**

A contribution map that records only novel proposals will systematically miss important forms of research judgement.

## Evidence status

The case is supported by the recorded project specification and frozen methods in the empirical manuscript.

## Boundary

The record shows researcher governance of these constraints. It does not establish that every technical detail of the subsequent rebuild was independently originated by the researcher.
