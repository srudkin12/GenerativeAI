# Reusable researcher tools

The templates are intentionally lightweight.

They are designed for **consequential decisions**, not complete surveillance of the research process.

## Templates

### [Reason-Provenance Record](../../templates/reason-provenance-record.md)

Use when a later contribution or attribution claim may depend on reconstructing how a consequential move entered the project.

### [AI-Assisted Research Decision Log](../../templates/ai-assisted-research-decision-log.md)

Use for major analytical, inferential or scope decisions.

### [Claim Challenge Record](../../templates/claim-challenge-record.md)

Use when a robustness check, comparator, reviewer or AI-assisted critique challenges a preferred claim.

### [Inference Revision Record](../../templates/inference-revision-record.md)

Use when a result remains numerically correct but its substantive interpretation changes, narrows or is withdrawn.

### [Researcher–AI Contribution Map](../../templates/researcher-ai-contribution-map.md)

Use to distinguish origination, evaluation, integration and interpretation across selected research episodes.

### [Public Release Screening Checklist](../../templates/public-release-screening-checklist.md)

Use before placing project-process material into a public repository, presentation or supplementary archive.
