# Practical workshop pathway

The seminar introduces the complete research architecture.

The workshops let participants practise one part of that architecture at a time using their own project or a supplied case.

Each workshop is designed around a **concrete take-away artefact**.

The default format below assumes approximately **two hours per workshop**, but each can be compressed to 90 minutes by shortening the group critique and debrief.

## Workshop 1 — Build the workflow

**Question:** How do I start a Generative-AI-assisted research project without surrendering the analytical object to the model?

**Participant output:** an AI-Assisted Research Charter and first Decision Log.

[Workshop 1 details](workshop-1-build-the-workflow.md)

## Workshop 2 — Challenge the research

**Question:** How do I use Generative AI to make my preferred interpretation smaller, stronger or more defensible rather than simply more elaborate?

**Participant output:** a Claim Challenge Record and Inference Revision Record.

[Workshop 2 details](workshop-2-challenge-the-research.md)

## Workshop 3 — From evidence to manuscript

**Question:** How do I turn verified evidence into a manuscript without allowing AI-assisted revision to change the authorised claim?

**Participant output:** a Claim-Preservation Contract, Evidence Ledger and Post-Revision Sufficiency Audit.

[Workshop 3 details](workshop-3-evidence-to-manuscript.md)

# How the workshops relate to the seminar

| Seminar movement | Workshop |
|---|---|
| Substantive problem and AI proposal generation | Workshop 1 |
| Specification and verification | Workshop 1 |
| Comparator challenge and inferential correction | Workshop 2 |
| Reason provenance and claim change | Workshop 2 |
| Claim authorisation and manuscript governance | Workshop 3 |
| Final-object inspection and release audit | Workshop 3 |

Participants do not need to attend all three, but the sequence is cumulative.

The repository should contain all templates needed to repeat the exercises later.
