# Public Release Screening Checklist

Use before publishing process evidence, worked cases, slides or archival material.

## Evidence status

- [ ] Every direct quotation has been checked against an authoritative source.
- [ ] Reconstructed conversation wording is paraphrased, not quoted.
- [ ] Current interpretation is distinguishable from contemporaneous evidence.
- [ ] Empirical claims refer to frozen/verified project evidence.

## Confidentiality and permissions

- [ ] No confidential peer-review material is included.
- [ ] No private collaborator material is included without permission.
- [ ] No personal identifiers are exposed unintentionally.
- [ ] Third-party images and screenshots have appropriate rights or have been removed.
- [ ] Publisher or journal confidentiality requirements have been checked.

## Research claims

- [ ] PISA is presented as a developmental illustration, not validation of the reason-provenance framework.
- [ ] AI-generated code/text is not described as empirical evidence.
- [ ] Methodological claims do not exceed the comparator and robustness evidence.
- [ ] Attribution claims are bounded to what the record can establish.
- [ ] Attribution is kept separate from normative endorsement of the division of labour.

## Metadata

- [ ] Author name is correct.
- [ ] Affiliation is Department of Social Statistics, University of Manchester.
- [ ] Repository version is correct.
- [ ] Citation metadata is correct.
- [ ] Licence and third-party rights statements are correct.
- [ ] DOI has been added after archival deposit, if applicable.
