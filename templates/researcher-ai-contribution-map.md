## Reading the map

Ask:

1. Which contributions changed the research trajectory?
2. Which contribution claims are supported by contemporaneous trace?
3. Which rely mainly on retrospective reconstruction?
4. Where could more than one contribution history still explain the final artefact?
5. Which attribution questions actually matter for authorship, disclosure, research integrity or interpretation?

## Important boundary

Clearer attribution does not establish that the observed division of labour is desirable.
