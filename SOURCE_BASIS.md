# Source Basis

The public resource distinguishes between transferable methods and the evidence used in worked examples.

## Worked examples

Selected worked examples are drawn from a PISA research project. Public descriptions are based on frozen manuscript and evidence records rather than reconstructed claims presented as contemporaneous fact.

## Constructed teaching material

The decision guide, crosswalk, field guide and explicitly constructed workflow/teaching scenarios are authored educational material. Their hypothetical events are not presented as empirical studies, historical episodes or evidence of AI effectiveness. They should never be used as sources for claims about the actual PISA project.

## Academic Writing Filter

The manuscript-governance material uses:

- *Academic Writing Filter* (October 2026 operational artefact);
- *From Writing Advice to Manuscript Governance: A Constrained-Transformation Framework for Academic Revision*.

## Public-release principle

Internal source material remains distinct from public interpretation.

Where wording has not been checked against an authoritative original, the public resource paraphrases rather than presents it as a quotation.
