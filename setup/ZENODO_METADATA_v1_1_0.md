**Description**

A practical educational and research-methods resource for social scientists using generative AI while retaining control over verification, inference, research decisions and claims of contribution. Version 1.1.0 expands the introductory route, problem-led decision guide, ten-step protocol crosswalk, field guide, constructed end-to-end research illustration and teaching activities. Seven bounded PISA research episodes illustrate governance problems without claiming validation of the framework. The resource includes reusable templates distinguishing contemporaneous decision traces from retrospective reconstruction, and preserves permissions and manuscript-governance boundaries.
