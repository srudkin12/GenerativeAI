## Overview

Version 1.1.0 extends the public resource for social scientists beginning to use generative AI. The new reader journey includes a bounded first GenAI encounter, task-led decision guide, protocol crosswalk, practical field guide, an entirely constructed research illustration and teaching activities. The seven existing PISA cases now provide more accessible explanations of their inferential lessons.

The revisions clarify contemporaneous decision recording, retrospective reconstruction, pre-use confidentiality checks, evidence verification, claim authorisation and manuscript-governance boundaries. The Cite page now foregrounds actual archived citations rather than the first-release deposit procedure.

## Evidence boundaries

The teaching scenarios are constructed; they do not report observational findings or actual AI transcripts. The PISA episodes are illustrative, not a validation of the reason-provenance framework. Raw private research records and confidential reviewer materials remain outside the public release. The Academic Writing Filter is linked, not republished or relicensed.
